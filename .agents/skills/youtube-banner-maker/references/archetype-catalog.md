# YouTube Channel Art: Archetype Design Catalog

A comprehensive design specification detailing the 7 high-converting channel art archetypes. Use this catalog to select visual systems, typography stacks, and styling rules.

---

## 1. Executive Authority (Boardroom, Advisory, C-Suite, M&A)

Ideal for high-ticket consultants, keynote speakers, private equity partners, and executive strategists.

- **Emotional Tone**: Sovereign, restrained, prestigious, institutional.
- **Palette**:
  - `bg_base`: `#0e1014` (Deep obsidian slate)
  - `card_bg`: `#15181f` (Charcoal plate with `stroke="#242b38"` at `1px`)
  - `accent_primary`: `#d4af37` (Warm 24K bullion gold)
  - `accent_secondary`: `#f5e6a8` (Champagne glow)
  - `text_primary`: `#f8f9fa` (Crisp alabaster)
  - `text_muted`: `#8e98a8` (Muted boardroom slate)
- **Typography**:
  - Headline: `Georgia`, `Playfair Display`, or `Baskerville` (Serif, 76px, weight 400, letter-spacing: `-1px`)
  - Subtitle: `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue` (Sans, 14px, weight 600, letter-spacing: `3px`, all-caps)
  - Kicker: `Inter` (Sans, 11px, weight 700, letter-spacing: `2.5px`, uppercase)
- **Signature Accents**:
  - Thin 1px horizontal gold keyline divider (`#d4af37`, opacity 0.5)
  - Credibility metrics stamp (e.g. `"$500M+ TRANSACTIONS CLOSED • PRIVATE EQUITY"`)
  - Elegant geometric monogram seal or watermark in the right visual container.

## 2. Scale Agency & High-Growth (B2B, DTC, Performance Marketing)

Ideal for growth agencies, media buyers, B2B SaaS marketers, and high-ticket agency founders.

- **Emotional Tone**: High-velocity, data-driven, hyper-lucrative, authoritative.
- **Palette**:
  - `bg_base`: `#08090d` (Dark carbon)
  - `card_bg`: `#0d111a` (Deep navy-tinted plate with `stroke="#1e293b"`)
  - `accent_primary`: `#00f0ff` (Hyper Cyan)
  - `accent_secondary`: `#3b82f6` (Electric Cobalt)
  - `text_primary`: `#ffffff` (Pure white)
  - `text_muted`: `#94a3b8` (Cool steel gray)
- **Typography**:
  - Headline: `Inter`, `Neue Haas Grotesk`, `system-ui` (Sans, 80px, weight 900, letter-spacing: `-2.5px`, uppercase)
  - Subtitle: `Inter` (Sans, 15px, weight 700, letter-spacing: `2px`, uppercase)
  - Metadata: Monospace (11px, weight bold, letter-spacing: `1.5px`)
- **Signature Accents**:
  - Velocity angle accents (linear keylines angled at 45° or cyan glow mesh)
  - Growth metric pills: `"8-FIGURE DTC"`, `"PAID MEDIA"`, `"CONVERSION"`
  - Verified safe-area pill with cyan status indicator.

## 3. Creative Director & Swiss Modernist (Branding, Design Systems, Typography)

Ideal for brand identity designers, UI/UX leads, typographers, and creative studio heads.

- **Emotional Tone**: Stark, curated, Bauhaus-informed, mathematically rigorous.
- **Palette**:
  - `bg_base`: `#050505` (Pitch black) or `#0a0a0a`
  - `accent_primary`: `#ff3333` (International Swiss Red) or `#ff5500` (Signal Safety Orange)
  - `grid_lines`: `#1f1f1f` (`stroke-width: 0.5px`, dashed or solid)
  - `text_primary`: `#ffffff`
  - `text_muted`: `#737373` (Neutral gray)
- **Typography**:
  - Headline: `Helvetica`, `Arial Black`, `Inter` (Sans, 82px, weight 900, letter-spacing: `-3px`, uppercase)
  - Subtitle: `Inter` (Sans, 14px, weight 500, letter-spacing: `2px`)
  - Micro-copy: `Space Mono`, `Courier` (Monospace, 10px, uppercase)
- **Signature Accents**:
  - Visible drafting grid lines and L-bracket registration marks (`+`, `⌜`, `⌟`)
  - Typographic asymmetric framing (e.g. vertical red rule beside brand name)
  - Minimalist geometric focal badge (abstract intersecting wireframe or typography glyph).

## 4. Solo Founder & Cyber Systems (Micro-SaaS, AI Engineers, Indie Hackers)

Ideal for solo software developers, machine learning researchers, indie builders, and open-source creators.

- **Emotional Tone**: Deep technical competence, autonomous, hacker-aesthetic, transparent.
- **Palette**:
  - `bg_base`: `#05070a` (Cyber obsidian)
  - `glow_mesh`: Radial mesh of `#0284c7` (Sky blue) and `#7c3aed` (Purple) at 20-30% opacity
  - `accent_primary`: `#38bdf8` (Terminal Cyan) or `#10b981` (Matrix Emerald)
  - `card_bg`: `#0b0f17` with subtle 1px border (`#1e293b`)
  - `text_primary`: `#ffffff`
  - `text_muted`: `#94a3b8`
- **Typography**:
  - Headline: Heavy Sans (`-apple-system`, `SF Pro Display`, 76px, weight 900)
  - Subtitle: Monospace or clean Sans (15px, weight 700, cyan gradient)
  - Tech Pills: `JetBrains Mono`, `Consolas`, `monospace` (11px, bold)
- **Signature Accents**:
  - Pulsing live ping dot (`<circle cx="14" cy="14" r="4" fill="#38bdf8">` with SVG `<animate>`)
  - Tech stack pills (`PYTHON`, `PYTORCH`, `LANGGRAPH`, `AGENTIC RAG`, `NEXT.JS`)
  - Terminal command readout: `"BUILDING TO $50K/MO • ZERO VC"`.

## 5. Creator Cutout & Breakout Frame (Personal Brands, Vloggers, Edu-Creators)

Ideal for creators whose face and personality are the primary distribution engine (YouTubers, vloggers, thought leaders).

- **Emotional Tone**: Charismatic, magnetic, approachable, cinematic.
- **Palette**:
  - `bg_base`: Moody monochrome or duotone background photograph (e.g. grayscale mountain reflection, foggy street)
  - `card_bg`: Translucent dark glass (`fill="#12161f" fill-opacity="0.7" stroke="#ffffff" stroke-opacity="0.15"`)
  - `accent_primary`: `#ff0000` (YouTube Signature Red) or `#f59e0b` (Amber gold)
  - `text_primary`: `#ffffff`
  - `text_muted`: `#d1d5db`
- **Typography**:
  - Headline: Punchy Bold Sans (`Impact`, `Montserrat`, `Inter`, 78px, weight 800)
  - Subtitle: Clean Sans (16px, weight 600, letter-spacing: `3px`, uppercase)
- **Signature Accents**:
  - Subject portrait popping outside the card frame with organic brush stroke halo or subtle drop shadow
  - Floating pill CTA: `"SUBSCRIBE"` with thumbs-up or bell badge icons
  - Split safe-zone composition: Left side text/identity, center/right side subject cutout.

## 6. E-Com Growth Lab (Shopify, Supply Chain, DTC Operators)

Ideal for e-commerce founders, 7-figure Amazon sellers, dropshipping educators, and supply chain analysts.

- **Emotional Tone**: High-margin, profitable, analytical, relentless execution.
- **Palette**:
  - `bg_base`: `#040d0a` (Midnight Pine)
  - `accent_primary`: `#10b981` (High-Growth Mint Emerald)
  - `accent_secondary`: `#34d399` (Spring Neon)
  - `card_bg`: `#081a15` (`stroke="#134e4a"`)
  - `text_primary`: `#ffffff`
  - `text_muted`: `#6ee7b7`
- **Typography**:
  - Headline: Modern Geometric Sans (`Inter`, `Poppins`, 76px, weight 900, tight tracking)
  - Subtitle: Uppercase Monospace or Sans (14px, weight 700, mint green)
  - Badges: Tabular data pills with revenue metrics (`"7-FIGURE BRANDS"`, `"CONVERSION & SCALE"`)
- **Signature Accents**:
  - Subtle upward trajectory graphic line
  - Safe-area verified badge in card corner
  - Financial data readout pills.

## 7. Minimalist Editorial Studio (Visual Essays, Architecture, Documentarians)

Ideal for visual essayists, documentary filmmakers, cultural commentators, and architectural critics.

- **Emotional Tone**: Intellectual, cinematic, timeless, museum-grade.
- **Palette**:
  - `bg_base`: `#141312` (Warm Espresso Dark)
  - `accent_primary`: `#c59b27` (Antique Gold leaf)
  - `accent_secondary`: `#e6ca65` (Pale gold highlight)
  - `card_bg`: `#1c1917` (`stroke="#44403c"`)
  - `text_primary`: `#fbf9f5` (Stone paper cream)
  - `text_muted`: `#a8a29e` (Warm parchment gray)
- **Typography**:
  - Headline: Classic Editorial Serif (`Georgia`, `Baskerville`, `Didot`, 78px, weight 400)
  - Subtitle: Clean Spaced Sans (15px, weight 600, letter-spacing: `4px`, uppercase)
  - Metadata: Monospace film aperture specs (11px, letter-spacing: `1.5px`)
- **Signature Accents**:
  - Fine-art window framing architectural geometry or still frame
  - Film aperture and camera markings (`"ARRI ALEXA MINI // 50MM T1.5"`, `"KODAK 5219"`)
  - Editorial volume stamp (`"VOL. IV"`), GPS coordinates, publication schedule.
