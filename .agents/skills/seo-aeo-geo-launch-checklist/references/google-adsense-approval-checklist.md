## Phase A — Baseline Eligibility (from Google's own eligibility page)

- [ ] **You are 18 or over** — Google only accepts applications from applicants 18+; if under 18, a
      parent/guardian must sign up using their own Google Account, and payments go to that adult
- [ ] **You own the content and can access its HTML source** — you must be able to access and modify
      the site's actual source code, not just view it
- [ ] **Content is your own** — original content you created, not solely aggregated/embedded from elsewhere
- [ ] If using a host partner (e.g. Blogger) or YouTube, you're going through the correct hosted-account
      path rather than a standard AdSense signup, and still complying with the same Program policies
      and Publisher Restrictions

## Phase B — Site Readiness (from "Make sure your site's pages are ready")

Google frames readiness as three explicit questions — answer each honestly before applying:

- [ ] **"What's special about your pages?"** — content is unique, original, and relevant; layout is
      inviting and helps visitors find what they're looking for; if a comment section exists, it's
      moderated for Program-policy compliance
- [ ] **"Do your pages have clear, easy-to-use navigation?"** — navigation bar/menu is aligned correctly,
      text is readable, dropdowns/interactive elements function correctly, and a user can quickly
      understand how to interact with the site
- [ ] **"Do your pages have unique and interesting content?"** — you contribute genuinely original
      material (specialist knowledge, original reviews, personal analysis), not primarily embedded
      videos or articles from other sites; **Google explicitly states ads may not be placed on sites
      with scraped or copyrighted content** — a direct policy violation risk, not just a quality nicety

## Phase C — AdSense Program Policy Compliance

- [ ] **No invalid clicks/impressions**: never click your own ads; no automated tools, robots, or
      deceptive software used to inflate clicks or impressions — including asking others to do so
- [ ] **No encouraged clicks (non-rewarded inventory)**: no "click the ads," "support us," "visit these
      links" language; no arrows/graphics directing attention to ads; no misleading images beside ads;
      no floating-box ad scripts; ads and content must each remain visually distinguishable from the other
- [ ] **Ad labels, if used, are accurate**: "Sponsored Links" / "Advertisements" are fine; "Favorite
      Sites" / "Today's Top Offers" are not
- [ ] **Traffic sources are clean**: no paid-to-click/paid-to-surf/autosurf/click-exchange programs; not
      promoted via unsolicited mass email; no ads triggered by toolbars or other software; no software
      that pops up windows, redirects users, or modifies browser settings
- [ ] **Ad placement is compliant**: no ads in pop-ups/pop-unders, inside emails, on private-communication
      screens, on non-content pages, on pages built specifically to show ads, on pages whose content/URL
      could be confused with Google branding, or on framed third-party content
- [ ] **Site behavior is clean**: no unwanted downloads initiated, no malware, no interfering pop-ups
- [ ] **No deceptive navigation**: ads are never placed where a user would mistake them for a menu,
      navigation, or download link (false streaming/download claims, links to nonexistent content, or
      misleading redirects all apply here)
- [ ] **ads.txt is correct** (if applicable): the domain's `ads.txt` file lists you as an authorized
      seller — Google-served ads are not permitted on a domain using `ads.txt` where you aren't listed

## Phase D — Google Publisher Policies (content, behavioral, privacy, standards)

### Content policies — none of the following may appear anywhere ads could serve
- [ ] Illegal content, or content infringing others' legal rights
- [ ] Copyright infringement or counterfeit-product sales
- [ ] Dangerous or derogatory content (hatred/discrimination on protected characteristics, harassment,
      threats or advocacy of self-harm/violence, extortion)
- [ ] Animal cruelty, or sale of products from endangered/threatened species
- [ ] Misrepresentative content: misleading claims about the publisher/creator/purpose; false
      affiliation/endorsement claims; demonstrably false claims undermining trust in elections; harmful
      health claims contradicting scientific consensus; climate-change denial contradicting consensus;
      deceptive practices (phishing, "get rich quick," concealed identity on political/social content);
      manipulated media on political/social matters
- [ ] Content enabling dishonest behavior (fake credentials/documents, hacking instructions, unauthorized
      tracking/spyware — excluding legitimate parental-monitoring tools)
- [ ] Sexually explicit content, non-consensual sexual themes, or AI-generated sexual content
- [ ] Compensated sexual acts or "mail order bride" content
- [ ] Adult themes disguised as family-friendly content
- [ ] Child sexual abuse or exploitation material (zero tolerance — reported to NCMEC/police, not just
      demonetized)

### Behavioral policies
- [ ] All information given to Google (account details, ads.txt, ad-request data) is materially accurate
      and complete — no obscured or misleading declarations
- [ ] Ads don't overlay/sit adjacent to navigation in a way that risks accidental clicks; ads don't
      severely interfere with content consumption; no "dead end" screens forcing an ad click to exit
- [ ] No ads on screens with no publisher content, low-value content, under-construction pages, or
      alert/navigation-only screens
- [ ] No ads appearing off-screen, in background processes, or where the user's attention is expected
      elsewhere — it must always be clear which content an ad is associated with
- [ ] No ads on screens with embedded/copied content lacking added commentary or value (ties directly
      to the scraped-content and intellectual-property-abuse policies)
- [ ] Screens don't carry more ads/paid promotional material than actual publisher content
- [ ] Primary content is in a supported AdSense language

### Privacy-related policies
- [ ] A privacy policy exists and clearly discloses data collection/sharing/usage from Google products
      and services, including cookie/web-beacon/IP-address use, across every platform used (web, app, etc.)
- [ ] No personalized/interest-based advertising targeted at known under-13 users, or on child-directed
      sites/sections, or using sensitive-category inferences (health, financial hardship, race/ethnicity,
      religion, criminal history, political affiliation, union membership, sexual orientation)
- [ ] Housing/employment/credit ad categories are not targeted by gender, age, parental status, marital
      status, or ZIP code (US/Canada) — narrow, defined exceptions exist for specific government job
      postings with a bona fide occupational qualification
- [ ] No cookies set/modified/deleted on Google's own domains
- [ ] No attempt to identify users or merge personally-identifiable data with previously non-PII data
      without explicit opt-in consent; no disaggregating Google's aggregated reporting data
- [ ] EU user consent policy compliance in place if serving EEA/UK/Switzerland users
- [ ] Precise device/location data collection (if any) is disclosed via interstitial/just-in-time notice,
      has express opt-in consent, is transmitted encrypted, and is covered in the privacy policy
- [ ] COPPA-covered sites/sections are tagged accordingly in Search Console/SDK, with no interest-based
      targeting of known under-13 users or child-directed content

### Requirements and other standards
- [ ] Complies with Search's own spam policies (see `bad-seo-practices-prevention-list.md` — this is a
      direct AdSense requirement, not just an SEO nicety)
- [ ] No "abusive experiences" as Google defines them (deceptive/hard-to-close ad experiences)
- [ ] No malware, ransomware, spyware, or unwanted software anywhere on pages serving ads
- [ ] Ads conform to the Coalition for Better Ads Standards
- [ ] Publisher is not operating from or on behalf of a sanctioned country/entity (Crimea, Cuba, the
      so-called Donetsk/Luhansk "republics," Iran, North Korea, or OFAC-restricted parties)

## Phase E — Publisher Restrictions (content that gets demonetized or under-monetized, not outright banned)

Content in these categories can still carry AdSense code, but will receive **less or no advertising**
since Google Ads and many other buyers won't bid on it. Flag it so the person can make an informed
decision, not because it blocks approval outright:

- [ ] Sexual content (nudity, sexually suggestive material, sexual fetish discussion, sexual
      entertainment/merchandise, affair-facilitation, sexual performance advice, sexual-enhancement
      product promotion)
- [ ] Shocking content (gruesome/graphic imagery, depicted violence, prominent obscene/profane language) —
      note the narrower exception carved out specifically for gameplay imagery
- [ ] Explosives, guns/gun parts, or other weapons (content promoting sale or giving assembly instructions)
- [ ] Tobacco, recreational drugs, alcohol sale/misuse, online gambling (with listed country exclusions),
      prescription drugs, or unapproved pharmaceuticals/supplements
- [ ] Apps removed from the Google Play Store for a policy violation
- [ ] Ads/content that obscure each other, even partially or briefly
- [ ] Video ad inventory not accurately declared (audibility, placement type) or using unsupported
      implementation methods, autoplay/sticky behavior outside the defined limits

## Phase F — Pre-Application Final Gate

- [ ] Every item in Phases A-D is a genuine PASS — these are hard eligibility/policy requirements, not
      optional polish
- [ ] Phase E items are identified and disclosed, if present, as expected-lower-monetization content —
      not a blocker to fix, but something to flag honestly
- [ ] This checklist has been cross-checked against `bad-seo-practices-prevention-list.md` and the main
      SKILL.md phase gates — an AdSense application on a site that fails the pre-launch indexing or
      spam-policy gates is applying prematurely
- [ ] The person understands: **passing every item here is not a guaranteed approval.** Google's actual
      review is manual/automated and can weigh factors (traffic quality, site history, regional policy)
      this checklist has no visibility into. This checklist certifies "no known, named policy violation
      is present" — never "approval guaranteed."

**Verdict format:** `ADSENSE READINESS: READY TO APPLY` / `READY WITH DISCLOSED RESTRICTIONS` (Phase E
items present but nothing blocking) / `NOT READY` (any Phase A-D item fails — list exactly which, with
evidence).
